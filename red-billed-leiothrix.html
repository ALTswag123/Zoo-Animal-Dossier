Red-Billed Leiothrix

Endangerment Status: Least Concern

Description

The Red-Billed Leiothrix is a somewhat small bird that lives in parts of Asia, especially southern China and the Himalayas.

Specifications

Scientific Classifications

Kingdom: Animalia
Species: Leiothrix lutea
Phylum: Chordata
Subphylum: Vertebrata
Class: Aves
Subclass: Neornithes
Order: Passeriformes
Parvorder: Passerida
Family: Leiothrichidae
Genus: Leiothrix

Weight and Size Range

Size ranges from 14-15.5cm long, weighing between 18-20 grams.

Habitat

Every type of jungle and forest, preferably dense pine forests.

Diet

Insects, fruits, and seeds.
