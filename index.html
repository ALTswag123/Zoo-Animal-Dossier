Harbor Seal

A species of seal that lives along coasts. They have
brown, white, tan, and grey coats with light or dark
spots. These seals can sometimes be found traveling over
100 miles upstream in freshwater rivers when searching
for food.[1]

Gila Monster

A venemous reptile located in northwestern Mexico
and the Southwestern United States. They are
moderately-sized lizards with orange/yellow and
black patterns along their bodies. The Gila Monster
enjoys humid climates and tends to live in shaded
areas near sources of water.[2][3]
