Gila Monster

Endangerment Status: Near Threatened

Description

The Gila Monster is a species of venemous reptile that lives in the Southwestern United States and nothwestern Mexico.

Specifications

Scientific Classifications

Kingdom: Animalia
Species: Heloderma suspectum
Phylum: Chordata
Subphylum: Vertebrata
Class: Reptilia
Order: Squamata
Suborder: Anguimorpha
Parvorder: Varanoidea
Family: Helodermatidae
Genus: Heloderma

Weight and Size Range

Adults range from 36-56cm long, weighing between 550-800 grams.

Habitat

Scrublands, succulent deserts, and Oak Woodlands.

Diet

Wide range of small animals like rodents, birds, snakes, lizards, and frogs, along with small eggs of other animals.
