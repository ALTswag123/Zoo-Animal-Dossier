Description

Weight and Size Range

Adults can reach up to 1.85 meters long. Weight ranges from 55kg to 168kg.

Habitat

Temperate coastal areas, rocky coasts, and sandy beaches.

Diet

A wide variety of fish. On occasion, they may also eat shelled aquatic life, octopi, or squid.
